# Dice Knight

**Status:** In Development · **Platforms:** TBA · **Release:** TBA

*Dice Knight* is a pixel-art roguelike where your entire arsenal is dice. Fight your way through a cursed land, build a bag of increasingly unhinged dice, and pray to the RNG gods — or stack the odds so you don't have to.

![Dice Knight — choosing a reward](assets/gameassets/diceknight/in-game-screenshots/Screenshot%202026-08-22%20121226-1920x1080.png)

## How it plays

- **Roll to fight.** Attacks, spells, and defenses all come from the dice in your bag.
- **Build your bag.** Buy, find, and upgrade dice — from a humble Basic die to Fire Thorn, Scorch, Tsunami, and Earthquake dice.
- **Push your luck.** Some dice are safe. Some are "5 or nothin". Choose wisely.
- **Branching runs.** Pick your path between fights, shops, and treasure — every run is different.

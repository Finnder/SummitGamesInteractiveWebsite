# Project Basecamp

**Status:** In Development · **Platforms:** TBA · **Release:** TBA

*Project Basecamp* is our first full title — a cozy survival adventure about building a home in the mountains, one supply run at a time.

## The pitch

You're the newest caretaker of a remote basecamp. Restore it, weather the seasons, and befriend the strange travelers who pass through on their way to the peak.

## Features (planned)

- Build and upgrade your basecamp, room by room
- A day/night and season cycle that actually changes how you play
- Travelers with their own stories, schedules, and requests
- No combat, no fail states — the mountain is the challenge
